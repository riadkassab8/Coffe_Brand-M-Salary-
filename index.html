Our Story

Born from Passion

SAFFRON began as a single roaster's dream — to bring the world's rarest coffee experiences to those who truly appreciate the art of the cup. Every bean we source carries a story of its land, its farmers, its journey.

From the highlands of Ethiopia to the volcanic slopes of Guatemala, we partner directly with farmers who share our obsession with quality, sustainability, and flavor complexity.
